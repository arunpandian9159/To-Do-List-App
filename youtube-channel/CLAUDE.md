# Tamil YouTube Channel: Project Context

Context handoff from a planning chat (last updated 2026-11-23). Read this before helping.

## Goal
A **faceless Tamil YouTube channel** made with AI video and **motion graphics built with Claude** (Remotion, the motion-graphics skills, AI visuals, an AI Tamil voice).

## Content priority (decided)
1. **Explainers**, 50%: brings search traffic and new viewers
2. **Mystery**, 30%: brings clicks, shares and binge-watching
3. **Tamil history**, 20%: builds loyal fans

Cadence: 2 long videos a week (8–12 min) plus about 5 Shorts a week cut from them.

Series names:
- **"ஏன்? எப்படி?"** (Why? How?): explainers
- **"மர்மம்"** (Mystery): mysteries
- **"தமிழர் வரலாறு"** (Tamil history): history

## Style rules
- One consistent look across all videos: same narrator voice, colour palette, fonts and intro sting.
- The hook goes in the first 5 seconds. Open on the most dramatic moment, with no slow intro.
- Natural spoken Tamil narration, not textbook Tamil. Use an ElevenLabs Tamil voice or the creator's own voice.
- Thumbnails show one bold question and one strong image.

## YouTube policy (must follow)
- Under YouTube's "inauthentic content" rules (clarified July 2026), channels of generic, repetitive or template-like AI videos can lose monetization **across the whole channel**.
- Every video needs a researched, fact-checked script and its own visuals. Never mass-produce near-identical videos.
- Don't use movie, trailer or news footage. Make original motion graphics instead, to avoid copyright claims.
- Avoid religious and communal disputes. The Thiruparankundram lamp row was skipped for this reason.
- For ongoing crime cases, say "police allege" or "போலீசார் கூறுவது" (according to police). Never state guilt. Use silhouettes, not real people's photos.

## Production workflow
1. Research, then write the Tamil script: hook, 3–4 story beats, twist, call to action.
2. Check the facts yourself.
3. Storyboard: plan a visual for every line.
4. Motion graphics in Remotion (maps, timelines, diagrams, animated text).
5. AI visuals for scenes that can't be drawn as graphics.
6. Voiceover.
7. Captions, music and sound effects.
8. Thumbnail and title.
9. Cut Shorts from the most surprising 30–50 seconds.

## Key facts gathered

**#1 Madurai Tanishq heist** (all police allegations, no conviction)
- Theft from a recently opened Tanishq showroom in Madurai, found on the morning of Nov 12, 2026. One report gives Sep 4 instead.
- Haul: 10.5–11.65 kg of gold plus about 1 kg of diamonds. Reported value ₹15–31 crore; sources conflict, so say so on screen.
- Prime accused: Nishanth, a YouTuber. Police allege he did it to clear the debts of his girlfriend Meenakshi, an Instagram influencer, during her house construction. He reportedly visited the showroom several times beforehand.
- Police allege Meenakshi lent him her motorcycle and revealed where the gold was hidden. About 5 kg was found buried at Madakulam tank on the outskirts of Madurai.
- Nishanth's father, V. Suresh, was also arrested. Meenakshi and Suresh are in judicial custody until Nov 29. Reports conflict on whether Nishanth was arrested (Nov 16) or is still absconding.
- One report says a tattoo exposed him.
- Sources: [TNM](https://www.thenewsminute.com/tamil-nadu/madurai-tanishq-heist-influencer-accuseds-father-arrested-5-kg-gold-recovered), [The Week](https://www.theweek.in/news/india/2026/11/14/indian-rupee31-crore-tanishq-robbery-tamil-influencer-meenakshi-arrested-hunt-on-for-youtuber-nishanth.html), [OdishaTV](https://odishatv.in/trending/rs-15-crore-heist-for-love-youtuber-robs-jewellery-showroom-to-help-girlfriend-clear-her-debts-tattoo-exposes-him-12832019)

**#3 Rice prices**
- Tamil Nadu has India's highest retail inflation: 7.29% in October 2026.
- Rice costs ₹61.66/kg in Tamil Nadu against ₹46.20 nationally (September).
- Stock limits: 2,000 t per wholesaler and 8 t per retail outlet, in force until Jan 31, 2027.
- Causes: El Niño rain shortfall, a weak Cauvery Delta crop, dependence on neighbouring states for fine rice, and alleged hoarding by millers. Millers deny there is a crisis.

**#4 TVK** won the most seats in the April 2026 assembly election. It's the first non-Dravidian largest party since the 1960s, and C. Joseph Vijay is Chief Minister.
